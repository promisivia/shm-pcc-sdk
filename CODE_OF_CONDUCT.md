# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in CXL-SDK a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive, technically grounded feedback;
- focusing discussion on the project and its users;
- acknowledging mistakes and learning from them; and
- respecting privacy, attribution, and responsible disclosure.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing
private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone represents CXL-SDK in
public, including issues, pull requests, discussions, events, and project
communication channels.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. If the concern
involves a maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting tools so that the report
can be handled independently. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect
reporter privacy where possible, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation depending
on severity and pattern.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not tolerated.

## Attribution

This policy is based on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
